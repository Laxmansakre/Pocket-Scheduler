Pocket Scheduler

A proper project schedule and Gantt chart — right in your browser. No install, no account, no monthly fee.

Built for small contractors, interior designers, and event planners who need real scheduling logic — WBS levels, predecessor/successor relationships (finish-to-start, lag/lead), automatic critical path — without the cost or setup of Primavera P6 or MS Project.

Open the live app →https://laxmansakre.github.io/Pocket-Scheduler/

Features
Activity table with WBS hierarchy, duration units (hours/days/weeks/months), and quantities
Predecessor & successor relationships with lead/lag, and automatic critical-path highlighting
Interactive Gantt chart with zoom, project calendar (working days & holidays), milestones, and fixed-date events
Multi-page, P6-style print/PDF output with a selectable timescale (Day/Week/Month/Quarter/Year)
Everything runs entirely in your browser — no server, no signup, no data collection. Your project stays on your device (with Save/Open .json for backups and sharing).
